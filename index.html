How to Play

  1. Tap an empty plot

    Find a '🌱' tile on your island and tap it to open the build menu.

  2. Choose a Building

    Click a building from the "Build" menu (like '🌸 Glimmerbloom') to start the puzzle.

  3. Solve the Puzzle

    Tap groups of 3 or more matching emoji to score points. Reach the "TARGET" score to win.

  4. Discover & Collect

    When you win, you'll discover a new island! Tap it to collect your rewards.

  5. Build & Grow

    Your new island will be placed on your home grid and its build timer will start. Wait for it to finish, then build more!
